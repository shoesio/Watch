# Watch — Apple TV build 71

## Browsing and card presentation

- Artwork and separate title/logo overlays share a tilting card surface, with foreground depth, a 7.5% focus enlargement, spring transition and a moving clipped highlight. Printed poster logos remain part of the photograph; no duplicate logo is added. Reduce Motion disables movement and retains a static focus indication.
- Hero crops align the image’s upper-third anchor to the frame’s upper-third anchor rather than centring vertically. This preserves more headroom without face detection.
- Continue Watching progress bars and captions have more space above the card’s bottom edge.
- Main browse shelves load lazily. Rounded card images are composited away from the main UI thread, coalesced and reused in the bounded artwork cache. These changes reduce avoidable work; no numerical performance improvement is claimed.

## Episode selection

- Show cards open show pages. The main show action is Choose episode; selecting an episode is the explicit playback action. Live channels still tune directly and films retain their detail-page playback action.
- All series is the default. The existing series filter remains available.
- Continue Watching and named seasonal episode cards pass their episode identity into navigation. The show page scrolls to and focuses that episode without playing it or restricting the list to its series.
- Episode cards show the series prefix only when viewing multiple series together, then the episode number, description, runtime and quality. The heading-to-description gap is four points. Repeated title/number metadata and the show-page Start over button are removed.

## Validation

Build 71 was built with Xcode and installed on the physical Apple TV. The user approved the focus-card styling and latest metadata spacing. Checks cover artwork title suppression, cinematic browsing and chronological ordering, selected-episode route round trips, malformed routes, metadata duplication, and playback timing/completion behaviour. Broad scrolling responsiveness and all episode-list focus cases remain subject to ordinary physical-device use.

This release changes the Apple TV client only. It does not alter provider sessions, Pi playback delivery, Samsung or Home Assistant behaviour. No private account data or downloaded media is included.

Rollback checkpoints: `checkpoint-appletv-build-71`, `checkpoint-appletv-build-63`, and `checkpoint-appletv-build-37`.
