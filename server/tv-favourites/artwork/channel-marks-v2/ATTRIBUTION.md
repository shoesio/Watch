# Live channel badge artwork

These are transparent channel identification marks, separate from landscape card artwork. Broadcaster trademarks remain with their owners.

BBC One, Two, Four and News: existing BBC channel vector wordmarks, rendered white on transparency. The badges use ONE, TWO, FOUR and NEWS without the BBC blocks.

Channel 4, E4, More4, Film4 and 4seven: existing channel logo vector paths from the broadcaster website branding previously retained in tv-favourites/artwork/live_channel4_*.svg. Extracted from the landscape card wrapper, rendered white on transparency; no card background retained. The exact vector source shapes are in Sources/.

Five family: official transparent white PNG channel logos provided by the broadcaster's image service, at https://api-images.channel5.com/otis/images/channel/{ID}/560x200.png . IDs: C5 (5), C7 (5USA), C6 (5STAR), C0 (5ACTION), C8 (5Drama). The 140x50 PNG channel-logo route is used by the official website; 560x200 supplies the larger source here. Transparent outer padding cropped; mark colours/shapes retained, including 5Drama's official reflection. Fetched 29 October 2026.

All marks are cropped to their visible bounds and aspect-fitted in the existing 64x32 point badge. This does not imply broadcaster endorsement.
